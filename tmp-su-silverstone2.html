Silverstone Circuit Overview

Stadium Name: Silverstone Circuit

Capacity: Approximately 164,000

Address: Silverstone Circuit, Towcester, Northamptonshire, NN12 8TN

Dress Code: Smart casual for most hospitality areas. Team merchandise is usually welcome, while smart footwear is recommended.

Audience: Formula 1 fans, motorsport enthusiasts, families, corporate guests, groups and premium experience seekers.

Silverstone Circuit is one of the most iconic venues in world motorsport and the home of the Formula 1 British Grand Prix. Known for its fast corners, passionate crowds and festival-style race weekend, Silverstone delivers one of the standout sporting experiences of the year.

About Silverstone Circuit

Year Founded: 1948

Major Events: Formula 1 British Grand Prix, MotoGP, Silverstone Festival, endurance racing and major motorsport events

Famous Corners: Copse, Maggotts, Becketts, Stowe and Club

Famous Drivers: Lewis Hamilton, Nigel Mansell, Damon Hill, Jenson Button and Lando Norris have all helped shape the British Grand Prix story.

Silverstone Circuit is a landmark venue in British sport. From its origins as a former airfield to its status as the home of the British Grand Prix, the circuit has become a must-visit destination for motorsport fans. A day at Silverstone is ideal for families, friends and corporate guests, combining elite racing with premium hospitality, entertainment and a passionate race-day atmosphere.

Silverstone Circuit highlights

  • Home of the Formula 1 British Grand Prix
  • One of the fastest and most historic circuits on the F1 calendar
  • Famous for corners including Copse, Maggotts and Becketts
  • A festival-style race weekend with entertainment across the venue
  • A standout hospitality destination for private and corporate guests

What are Silverstone Circuit events like?

Silverstone Circuit events combine speed, atmosphere and occasion. During the British Grand Prix, you will experience the sound of Formula 1 cars at full pace, passionate fans across the circuit and a premium event environment built around one of the biggest weekends in British sport.
