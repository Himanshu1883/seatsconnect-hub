Formula 1 overview

Championship: FIA Formula One World Championship

Founded: 1950

Venues: Multiple permanent, temporary and street circuits worldwide

Capacity: Varies considerably by circuit and event configuration

Address: Check the individual Grand Prix event page for the correct circuit address

Dress code: Determined by the circuit, hospitality facility and package

Audience: Suitable for motorsport fans, families, friends, groups and corporate guests, subject to event-specific age restrictions

Formula 1 is the highest level of international motor racing. The championship visits established circuits, city-centre street tracks and modern purpose-built venues across Europe, Asia-Pacific, the Americas and the Middle East.

Every circuit offers a different experience. You could watch cars race through the streets of Monaco, see them tackle the sweeping corners of Silverstone or Spa-Francorchamps, or enjoy a night race beneath the lights in Singapore, Las Vegas or Abu Dhabi.

What is new for Formula 1 in 2027?

The 2027 season will feature several important venue changes.

The Portuguese Grand Prix will return at the Autódromo Internacional do Algarve in Portimão. The circuit previously hosted Formula 1 in 2020 and 2021 and is known for its significant elevation changes and technical layout.

The Turkish Grand Prix will also return at Istanbul Park. The circuit is recognised for its demanding sequence of corners, including the long, multi-apex Turn Eight.

About Formula 1

Year founded: 1950

Major honours: Formula 1 Drivers’ World Championship and Formula 1 Constructors’ World Championship

Notable rivalries: Team and driver rivalries change from season to season, with long-standing competition involving names such as Ferrari, McLaren, Mercedes and Red Bull

Famous drivers: Juan Manuel Fangio, Jim Clark, Niki Lauda, Alain Prost, Ayrton Senna, Michael Schumacher, Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen

Formula 1 has developed from a predominantly European championship into a worldwide sporting series. Its identity is shaped by technical innovation, driver skill, team strategy and the contrasting demands of each circuit.

The championship entered a new technical era in 2026, with revised power units, increased electrical energy, advanced sustainable fuel and active aerodynamic systems. Cadillac also joined the grid as Formula 1’s 11th team.

A Grand Prix can provide an excellent day out or full weekend for families, friends and motorsport supporters. Hospitality also offers companies a distinctive setting for recognising employees, building client relationships and bringing guests together.

Formula 1 highlights

  • The Formula 1 World Championship has been contested since 1950.
  • The season brings together permanent circuits, temporary venues and street races.
  • Drivers compete for the individual World Championship, while teams contest the Constructors’ Championship.
  • Formula 1 introduced a major new generation of technical regulations in 2026.
  • Cadillac became the championship’s 11th team in 2026.
  • Portugal and Türkiye are set to return to the Formula 1 calendar in 2027.

What are Formula 1 race weekends like?

A Formula 1 weekend is about much more than the Grand Prix itself. Most events include practice sessions, qualifying, support races, entertainment and the main race. Selected rounds use a Sprint format, creating an additional competitive session during the weekend.

The experience differs greatly by destination. Traditional circuits place you close to recognisable corners and long-established grandstands, while street races bring Formula 1 into the centre of major international cities.

Hospitality gives you a comfortable base between sessions, which can be particularly valuable during a long day at the circuit. Depending on your package, you may be able to dine, meet other guests, follow live timing or watch parts of the action from a terrace or lounge.
